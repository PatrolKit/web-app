How it works

PatrolKit is invite-only. Your patrol director controls access, keeping your patrol's data secure and your team in sync.

01

Get invited

Your patrol director adds you to PatrolKit. You'll receive an email invitation to create your account and join your patrol.

02

Get organized

Access your patrol's schedule, log your hours, and stay on top of patrol operations from any device — phone, tablet, or desktop.

03

Stay connected

Shift reminders, patrol-wide notifications, and real-time smart TV announcements keep your whole patrol on the same page all season long.

  • Live staffing view so directors always know who's on hill
